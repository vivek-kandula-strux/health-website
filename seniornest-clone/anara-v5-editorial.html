FAQ 10 · Questions

Answers to the questions HR leaders ask first.

Q. What's the minimum contract length?
A. Contracts are flexible. New clients can start with a six-month pilot — including an on-site clinic if relevant — to evaluate fit before a longer commitment.
Q. How is employee health data protected?
A. We follow strict data-protection standards aligned with Indian regulations. Individual health information is never shared with the employer — only aggregated, anonymized insights for planning workforce programs.
Q. Can we customize the package?
A. Yes — services are modular. Pick on-site clinics, telehealth, second opinions, care coordination, or preventive programs in any combination that fits your workforce.
Q. How do you keep pricing competitive on quality care?
A. We negotiate rates with premium providers, including Apollo Hospitals and other top networks, and a dedicated clinical team reviews recommendations to avoid unnecessary procedures.
Q. Can employees see doctors outside the Anara network?
A. Yes. We recommend the in-network providers for the best quality-to-cost balance, but the concierge team will coordinate care with outside doctors when an employee prefers.
Q. How does the total cost compare to traditional corporate plans?
A. For most clients, the all-in cost lands at or below traditional plans — driven by negotiated rates, prevention, and clinically reviewed care plans that avoid unnecessary spend.
