India runs on its corporates. Corporates run on their people. Workforce healthcare should act like it.

The thesis

Most workforce health programs in India measure activity — drives run, mailers sent, vouchers issued. None of those numbers add up to a healthier employee.

Anara was built on a different bet: if the care comes to where work happens, in the format people already use, the outcomes follow. On-site clinics. WhatsApp follow-up. India's existing health stack. No new app, no portal, no chasing.

FAQ

What HR & procurement ask first.

Will Anara interfere with our existing TPA or broker?
No. Anara works alongside your GMC and TPA, not instead of them. Your broker relationship doesn't change.
Will this add work for our HR team?
Your HR lead confirms the date and shares the employee list. We handle equipment, doctors, reminders, lab coordination, and the post-clinic report. Most HR contacts tell us we're the one healthcare program they don't have to manage.
What participation rate can we realistically expect?
85%+ across client sites. We come during working hours, to your office, with everything set up. Industry average for externally-run programs is 40–50%.
Can you cover our employees' parents?
Yes — Care Managers do weekly home visits across our service cities, plus on-demand telehealth. They manage medications, lab coordination, hospital accompaniment, and send real-time WhatsApp updates to the employee.
What does executive health look like for senior leadership?
A dedicated session at your office — cardiovascular risk profiling, executive stress assessment, full blood panel, 1:1 consultation. No waiting. Full confidentiality. Leaders get a personal summary and a 12-month preventive plan.
How does billing work?
Most clients structure payment through their OPD reimbursement budget — typically ₹5,000–15,000 per employee per year, already sitting in your GMC. Per-employee per-year or per-engagement, whichever fits your procurement structure. We'll walk you through the options on the first call.
